Retro Cassette: Tape Player

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Your music on a cassette: spinning reels, lossless audio, lyrics and mixtapes.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Retro Cassette turns your music library into a modern walkman. The reels spin with the song's position, the tape winds from one side to the other and every track gets a label, in a clean, minimal look.

🎧 PLAY YOUR MUSIC
• Plays the audio files on your device — no internet, no account
• Lossless audio: FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF — with an HQ badge and the track format (e.g. FLAC · 24-bit · 96 kHz)
• Gapless playback, smooth fades between tracks and even volume (ReplayGain)
• Built-in equalizer with bass boost
• Adjustable speed (0.5× to 2×) and pitch
• Resumes where you left off, and when headphones connect

📼 LOOKS LIKE A REAL TAPE
• Upright cassette with 3D reels and piano keys
• Normal, Chrome and Metal tapes, label colors and a handwritten playlist name
• Side A / Side B: the queue becomes a two-sided tape that flips with an animation
• Optional tape hiss, wow & flutter and the clack of the keys
• Player background in the cover's colors

📚 ORGANIZE
• Home and Library with playlists, albums, artists and folders
• Create playlists and drag to reorder
• Favorites, Most played, Haven't heard in a while and Recently added
• Search by title, artist, album — and inside lyrics
• Edit tags and covers right in the file, with online cover search
• Back up playlists and favorites

🎤 LYRICS
• Synced lyrics that follow the song — tap a line to jump there
• From the file itself, .lrc files or online (LRCLIB)

✨ AND MORE
• Sleep timer that fades the volume out
• Home-screen widget with spinning reels
• Android Auto and watch controls
• Listening stats with a monthly recap
• Share your mixtape as a cassette image
• Last.fm scrobbling (via Pano Scrobbler or Simple Last.fm Scrobbler)
• Dark and light themes; English, Portuguese and Spanish

No ads. No tracking. Your music stays on your device.
